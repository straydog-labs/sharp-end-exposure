What this means

Climbers in the Panic zone are attempting routes beyond their current psychological readiness. SEE makes this visible before it becomes an incident — not by telling climbers what to do, but by helping them notice what they're experiencing.

Sharp End Exposure by Straydog Labs
"A climb log that tracks your body, not just your grades."
Straydog Labs LLC — Nashville, TN — Founded 2026
The Problem
What exists
  • Grades
  • Volume
  • Sends
  • Training load
What's missing
  • Activation state
  • Fear response
  • Somatic signals
  • Fall experience
The Product

SEE tracks somatic response to climbing across sessions over time. It builds self-awareness until the pre-climb check-in becomes automatic. It is a noticing tool, not a decision-maker — the climber interprets the data.
